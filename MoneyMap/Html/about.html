About MoneyMap

Built for people who want to understand their money

MoneyMap is a personal finance tracker designed to be simple, clear, and honest about your financial habits — no subscriptions, no algorithms, no noise.

What it is

A tool that gives you the full picture

In today's world, managing personal finances can feel overwhelming. MoneyMap simplifies the process by letting you log income and expenses, set category budgets, and visualize where your money actually goes — all in one clean interface.

Whether you're tracking monthly groceries or planning yearly savings, MoneyMap gives you the clarity to make better financial decisions.

What MoneyMap can do

Every feature is built around one goal — helping you understand your money.

₹

Income Tracking

Log your monthly income and keep it as your baseline. Every calculation starts from here.

📊

Category Budgets

Set spending limits for Food, Bills, EMI, and Entertainment. Know your limits before you exceed them.

📈

Visual Charts

Pie and bar charts update in real time as you add expenses. See your budget allocation at a glance.

🏦

Savings Forecast

See your estimated monthly and yearly savings automatically calculated from your income and expenses.

📄

PDF Reports

Export a full financial summary as a PDF — income, expenses, savings, and charts included.

🔐

Secure Login

Your account is protected with Firebase Authentication. Your data stays private to you.
